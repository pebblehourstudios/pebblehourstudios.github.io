Pixel Pour

Sand-sort puzzle

Pixel Pour is a cozy sand-sort puzzle. Tap a jar and colored sand pours into the frame, grain by grain, until a pixel-art picture appears. Finished pictures collect into themed albums across 100 levels, with more on the way, and there is no timer to rush you.

Letterpaths

Word puzzle

Letterpaths is a calm anagram word game. Trace a path through the letters and each solved word reveals more of an AI-assisted watercolor painting. An original piano soundtrack plays along, and Sprint mode is there when you want a quicker round.
