Pixel Pour privacy policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

Pixel Pour ("the game") is an Android game from Trucent Enterprises, LLC, doing business as Pebble Hour Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the game does with information.

The short version

  • This version of the game doesn't connect to the internet. It sends nothing to us or to anyone else.
  • No accounts. The game doesn't ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device.
  • No real ads and no real purchases yet. Ad and purchase screens in this version are simulated for testing. Nothing is charged.
  • Before we add real ads, analytics or purchases, we'll update this policy first.

What the game stores on your device

The game saves the following in its private storage on your phone. This stays on your device. We never receive it.

  • Progress: levels, coins, boosters, lives and settings
  • A short log of recent in-game events

The game also lets you save a postcard image. If you share an image, it goes where you choose to send it, not to us.

What we don't collect

The game doesn't collect, receive or share your personal information. It has no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software. It is built with the Godot game engine, and the game works without an internet connection.

Settings includes an Analytics switch. In this version it does nothing, because the game can't connect to the internet.

Progress, reinstalling and new phones

There's no account or cloud save, and the game doesn't use Android's backup. If you uninstall the game, clear its storage, use Reset progress, or move to a new phone, your progress starts over. We can't restore it.

Ads and purchases in this version

The ads and purchases you may see in this version are simulated for testing. No ad company is involved and no payment is made.

Google Play

If you get the game from Google Play, Google handles your download, updates and any information it collects about you under Google's own privacy policy. We don't control that.

Children

The game doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it sends no information to us. If you have questions, contact us.

Deleting your information

Because the game doesn't send your information to us, we have nothing to delete. To remove what the game stores on your phone, uninstall the game or clear its storage in Android Settings.

Your privacy rights

Because the game doesn't send us information about you, we don't hold personal information about you through the game to access, correct or delete.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date. Before we add real ads, analytics or purchases, we'll update this policy first and tell players in the game or in the Play listing.
