Pixel Pour privacy policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Pixel Pour ("the game") is developed by Trucent Enterprises, LLC, doing business as Pebble Hour Studios ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the game. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. We wrote it to be short and readable.

1. The short version

  • No accounts. We don't ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.
  • Your progress is saved on your device: levels, stars, coins, boosters, albums, and settings.
  • Analytics is off by default. Only if you turn it on in Settings are anonymous gameplay events (for example "level 12 started / won / lost, how long it took") sent to Google Firebase so we can make levels fairer and more fun. Analytics never collects your device's Advertising ID.
  • Ads are shown by our advertising partner. That partner collects device information to show and measure ads.
  • Purchases go through Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.

3. Third parties

  • Google Firebase (Analytics and Remote Config). Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Our advertising partner. This partner shows and measures ads, and processes device information under its own privacy policy. Any other partner involved in serving those ads does the same under its own policy.
  • Google Play (purchases). Google Payments Privacy Notice. Google states that your payment information "will not be shared with app developers" (Google Play Help).

These companies process data under their own policies and, where they act on our behalf, under their terms with us.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases such as Remove Ads and coin packs are handled by Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment. We receive only what's needed to confirm and deliver the purchase: the product ID, a purchase token, and the order details. We never receive your card number or billing address.

5. Ads and your choices

  • The game shows rewarded ads, which you choose to watch for a bonus, and interstitial ads that appear only after a level ends, not during play, and never after you buy Remove Ads.
  • Personalized vs non-personalized. Personalized ads use your Advertising ID and past activity to pick ads. Non-personalized ads don't use your history for targeting, but still use identifiers for frequency capping, fraud prevention, and reporting.
  • EEA, UK, and Switzerland. We'll ask for your consent through a Google-certified consent tool before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the game's settings.
  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID. On Android, go to Settings > Privacy > Ads (wording varies by device) and choose "Reset advertising ID" or "Delete advertising ID". Deleting it stops apps from using it for personalized ads. Google's help page explains these controls.
  • US state privacy rights. You can opt out of personalized ads with the controls above.

6. Children's privacy

Pixel Pour is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we'll delete it.

7. Data retention

  • On your device: game progress stays until you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Firebase Analytics: we keep user-level and event-level analytics data for the shortest period that meets our needs (Google Analytics data retention). Aggregated, non-identifying reports may be kept longer.
  • Firebase installation IDs: kept until deleted. After deletion, Firebase removes them from live and backup systems within 180 days (Firebase privacy information).
  • Ads and Google Play: retention is set by Google and our advertising partner, under their policies.

8. Your choices and deletion requests

  • Turn analytics off at any time in Settings. Data stops being sent from that point.
  • Uninstalling the game removes all local data.
  • To ask us to delete analytics data linked to your install, email support@pebblehourstudios.com with the subject "Pixel Pour data deletion". Because we don't have accounts, we may ask for your app-instance ID if the game shows one in Settings, so we can find your data. We'll respond within 30 days.

9. Security

Data sent by Firebase and our advertising partner is encrypted in transit (TLS). Firebase describes this encryption for the analytics data it handles. We limit access to our analytics console to the people who need it. No system is perfectly secure, but we collect as little as possible.

10. International users

We're based in Georgia, USA. Google and our partners may process data in the United States and other countries. Google states that it complies with the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and its UK and Swiss extensions (Firebase privacy information).

  • EEA/UK (GDPR): our legal bases are consent for analytics and personalized ads, and legitimate interests and contract for delivering purchases and basic, non-personalized ad serving with fraud prevention. You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, object, and withdraw consent. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.
  • California and other US states (CCPA/CPRA and similar laws): we don't sell personal information. You can ask to know or delete the information we hold, and you can opt out of personalized ads ("sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising) using section 5. We won't treat you differently for using these rights.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the game or in the Play listing.
