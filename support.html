Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

Your progress is saved on your device. There's no account to sign in to, so your place stays on the phone or tablet you were playing on.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

Neither Pixel Pour nor Letterpaths has cloud save or Android backup, so progress can't move to a new phone.

What if I reinstall or clear app data?

Reinstalling the app, clearing its data in Android settings, or moving to a new phone erases the progress stored on that device. We can't restore it.

How do I ask you to delete data?

Neither game sends your progress or settings to us, so we don't hold game data about you.

To delete what a game stores on your phone, uninstall it or clear its storage in Android Settings (Settings > Apps > the game > Storage > Clear storage; wording varies by device). Pixel Pour also has a Reset progress button in Settings.
