Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

Progress is saved on your device. There is no account to sign in to, so your place in the game stays on the phone or tablet you were playing on.

What if I reinstall or clear app data?

Reinstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, erases the progress stored on that device. We can't restore it.

How do I ask you to delete data?

Email support@pebblehourstudios.com and name the game. Letterpaths stores nothing on our side: progress and settings stay on your device, and they are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Pixel Pour keeps progress on your device as well. If you turned analytics on, the Pixel Pour privacy policy explains how to ask us to delete analytics data linked to that install.
