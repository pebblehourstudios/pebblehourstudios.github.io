Letterpaths privacy policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Letterpaths ("the game") is developed by Trucent Enterprises, LLC, doing business as Pebble Hour Studios ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the game. This page says, in plain language, what the game does with information.

Offline, and nothing collected

Letterpaths runs fully offline. The app does not request the Android internet permission.

The game collects no personal data. It does not share personal data, and it does not sell personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs.

Saved on your device only

Game progress and settings are stored only on your device, in local app storage. We never receive them.

That information is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Children

Letterpaths is not directed at children under 13.

If this changes

If the game ever adds a feature that changes this policy, such as purchases, we will update this page and change the effective date.
