Letterpaths privacy policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

Letterpaths ("the game") is an Android game from Trucent Enterprises, LLC, doing business as Pebble Hour Studios ("we", "us"). This page says, in plain language, what the game does with information.

What stays on my device?

  • Your progress, scores, collected paintings and settings. They're saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. We never receive them.
  • Letterpaths runs offline. The app doesn't ask Android for internet permission, so it can't send anything over the internet.
  • The game doesn't use Android backup.

What we don't collect

The game doesn't collect, receive, share or sell personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

It has no advertising, analytics or tracking software.

The game displays its screens using Android System WebView, a part of your phone managed by Android, not by us. The game blocks it from loading anything from the internet.

Reinstalling and new phones

There's no account or cloud save. If you uninstall the app, clear its storage or move to a new phone, your progress starts over. We can't restore it.

Deleting your information

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android Settings. Because nothing is sent to us, we have nothing else to delete.

Emailing support

If you email support@pebblehourstudios.com, we receive your email address and what you write, and we use it to reply. This is separate from the game, which sends us nothing.

Google Play

If you install Letterpaths through Google Play, Google handles your download and updates under Google's own privacy policy.

Children

The game doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it sends nothing to us.

If this changes

If a future version adds ads, analytics, purchases or any online feature, we'll update this page and the effective date first.
